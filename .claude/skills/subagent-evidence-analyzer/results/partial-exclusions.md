# Partial Evidence Exclusions

Cases where courts excluded some evidence but retained other portions.

---

## 1. I Kž 181/10-3 (Vrhovni sud)

**Date:** 2010-05-12
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Bedroom search records | EXCLUDED |
| Bathroom search records | EXCLUDED |
| Kitchen search records | RETAINED |

### Reason
Witnesses testified they were **not continuously present** during bedroom and bathroom searches. Kitchen search had proper witness presence throughout.

### Principle
Witness presence must be **continuous** - brief absences during search can invalidate that portion.

## 3. Kž-270/2022-10 (Županijski sud u Bjelovaru)

**Date:** 2022-09-21
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Audio recording of conversation | EXCLUDED |
| Photographs from search | RETAINED |
| Witness statements | RETAINED |

### Reason
Audio recording made **without consent or judicial authorization** violated Article 10(2) ZKP. Photographs derived from lawful warrant-based search.

### Key Distinction
Private recordings require either:
- Consent of recorded party, OR
- Judicial authorization under Art. 332 ZKP

## 4. 5 Kž-720/2017-3 (Županijski sud u Zagrebu)

**Date:** 2017-09-05
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Call duration/frequency data | EXCLUDED |
| IMEI numbers | EXCLUDED |
| Phone owner identification | RETAINED |

### Reason
**Article 339 ZKP** requires judge's order for telecommunication metadata. Paragraphs 3-4 of police report (call logs, IMEI) obtained without such order.

### Principle
Phone subscriber identity ≠ call metadata. Subscriber info may be obtainable without special order; call details require judicial authorization.

## Pattern Analysis

### What Gets Excluded
- Audio recordings without consent/authorization
- Telecom metadata without judge's order
- Search portions without continuous witness presence
- Statements taken with unqualified counsel
- Specific items seized without proper individual authorization

### What Gets Retained
- Evidence from separate lawful searches
- Witness statements obtained independently
- Photographs from properly authorized procedures
- Items covered by valid search orders
