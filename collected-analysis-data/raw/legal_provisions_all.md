# ZKP Legal Provisions Quick Reference

Key provisions for evidence exclusion under Croatian Criminal Procedure Act.

---

## Core Exclusion Rule

### Article 10 ZKP
**Prohibition on Unlawfully Obtained Evidence**

```
(1) Evidence obtained through violation of fundamental rights guaranteed
    by Constitution, international law, or this Act shall not be used
    in criminal proceedings.

(2) Evidence obtained in violation of provisions of this Act shall
    also be considered unlawful if:
    1. obtained through torture, cruel treatment, or violation of
       human dignity
    2. obtained in violation of defendant's rights to defense
    3. obtained through procedural violations expressly designated
       by this Act as rendering evidence unlawful
    4. derived from unlawful evidence (fruit of poisonous tree)
```

---

## Search & Seizure Provisions

### Article 240 ZKP
**Search Order Requirements**
- Written order required
- Must specify: location, subject, executing authority
- Oral orders permitted only in emergencies (followed by written confirmation)

### Article 242 ZKP
**Search Authorization**
- Issued by investigating judge
- Must contain: grounds for suspicion, purpose, scope
- Deficient authorization = unlawful evidence

### Article 247(3) ZKP
**Nighttime Search Exception**
- Nighttime searches generally prohibited
- Exception: defendant specifically requests it
- Must be documented

### Article 250 ZKP
**Exhaustive Exclusion Grounds**
Search evidence unlawful ONLY if:
- No proper order/authorization
- Order not properly served
- Required persons not present
- Nighttime prohibition violated

### Article 254 ZKP
**Witness Presence**
- Two adult witnesses required
- Must be present throughout search
- Cannot be police officers or parties

---

## Testimony Provisions

### Article 285(3) ZKP
**Privileged Witness Warnings**
- Close relatives may refuse testimony
- Warning must be given AND recorded
- Witness response must be documented
- Failure to record = unlawful evidence

### Article 286 ZKP
**Witness Rights Notification**
- Right to refuse self-incrimination
- Right to counsel in certain cases

### Article 344(4) ZKP
**Hearsay Restrictions**
- Witness cannot testify to what they heard from others
- Police testimony about defendant's statements restricted

---

## Surveillance Provisions

### Article 332 ZKP
**Special Investigative Measures**
- Wiretapping requires judge's order
- Order must specify: target, duration, grounds
- Results usable only for specified offense

### Article 339 ZKP
**Telecommunication Data**
- Call logs, IMEI, metadata require judge's order
- Subscriber identity may be obtainable separately
- Unauthorized access = unlawful evidence

---

## Defense Rights

### Article 177 ZKP
**Right to Counsel**
- Mandatory defense for serious offenses
- Counsel presence required for identification procedures
- Absence during critical proceedings = potential exclusion

### Article 10(2)(2) ZKP
**Defense Violations**
Evidence unlawful if obtained "in violation of defendant's rights to defense"

---

## Derived Evidence

### Article 331(2) ZKP / Article 10(2)(4) ZKP
**Fruit of the Poisonous Tree**
- Evidence derived from unlawful evidence is also unlawful
- "Plodovi otrovne voćke" doctrine
- Exception: independent source

---

## Constitutional Provision

### Article 34 Ustav RH
**Home Inviolability**
```
(1) The home shall be inviolable.
(2) Only the court may, upon a written order, authorize entry and
    search of the home.
(3) Resident has right to be present during search.
```

---

## Police Powers

### Article 74 ZPP (Law on Police Duties)
**Entry Requirements**
- Written authorization required for home entry
- Exceptions: emergency, hot pursuit, consent
- Violation = unlawful evidence

### Article 75(1)(2) ZPP
**Inspection Powers**
- Visual inspection permitted in limited circumstances
- Distinct from formal search
- Does not authorize home entry

### Article 211.a ZKP
**Open Area Exception**
- Open/natural spaces not protected as "home"
- Courtyards, fields, etc. may not require warrant
- Applies to "natural space" under paragraph 2

---

# ZKP Legal Provisions Quick Reference

Key provisions for evidence exclusion under Croatian Criminal Procedure Act.

---

## Core Exclusion Rule

### Article 10 ZKP
**Prohibition on Unlawfully Obtained Evidence**

```
(1) Evidence obtained through violation of fundamental rights guaranteed
    by Constitution, international law, or this Act shall not be used
    in criminal proceedings.

(2) Evidence obtained in violation of provisions of this Act shall
    also be considered unlawful if:
    1. obtained through torture, cruel treatment, or violation of
       human dignity
    2. obtained in violation of defendant's rights to defense
    3. obtained through procedural violations expressly designated
       by this Act as rendering evidence unlawful
    4. derived from unlawful evidence (fruit of poisonous tree)
```

---

## Search & Seizure Provisions

### Article 240 ZKP
**Search Order Requirements**
- Written order required
- Must specify: location, subject, executing authority
- Oral orders permitted only in emergencies (followed by written confirmation)

### Article 242 ZKP
**Search Authorization**
- Issued by investigating judge
- Must contain: grounds for suspicion, purpose, scope
- Deficient authorization = unlawful evidence

### Article 247(3) ZKP
**Nighttime Search Exception**
- Nighttime searches generally prohibited
- Exception: defendant specifically requests it
- Must be documented

### Article 250 ZKP
**Exhaustive Exclusion Grounds**
Search evidence unlawful ONLY if:
- No proper order/authorization
- Order not properly served
- Required persons not present
- Nighttime prohibition violated

### Article 254 ZKP
**Witness Presence**
- Two adult witnesses required
- Must be present throughout search
- Cannot be police officers or parties

---

## Testimony Provisions

### Article 285(3) ZKP
**Privileged Witness Warnings**
- Close relatives may refuse testimony
- Warning must be given AND recorded
- Witness response must be documented
- Failure to record = unlawful evidence

### Article 286 ZKP
**Witness Rights Notification**
- Right to refuse self-incrimination
- Right to counsel in certain cases

### Article 344(4) ZKP
**Hearsay Restrictions**
- Witness cannot testify to what they heard from others
- Police testimony about defendant's statements restricted

---

## Surveillance Provisions

### Article 332 ZKP
**Special Investigative Measures**
- Wiretapping requires judge's order
- Order must specify: target, duration, grounds
- Results usable only for specified offense

### Article 339 ZKP
**Telecommunication Data**
- Call logs, IMEI, metadata require judge's order
- Subscriber identity may be obtainable separately
- Unauthorized access = unlawful evidence

---

## Defense Rights

### Article 177 ZKP
**Right to Counsel**
- Mandatory defense for serious offenses
- Counsel presence required for identification procedures
- Absence during critical proceedings = potential exclusion

### Article 10(2)(2) ZKP
**Defense Violations**
Evidence unlawful if obtained "in violation of defendant's rights to defense"

---

## Derived Evidence

### Article 331(2) ZKP / Article 10(2)(4) ZKP
**Fruit of the Poisonous Tree**
- Evidence derived from unlawful evidence is also unlawful
- "Plodovi otrovne voćke" doctrine
- Exception: independent source

---

## Constitutional Provision

### Article 34 Ustav RH
**Home Inviolability**
```
(1) The home shall be inviolable.
(2) Only the court may, upon a written order, authorize entry and
    search of the home.
(3) Resident has right to be present during search.
```

---

## Police Powers

### Article 74 ZPP (Law on Police Duties)
**Entry Requirements**
- Written authorization required for home entry
- Exceptions: emergency, hot pursuit, consent
- Violation = unlawful evidence

### Article 75(1)(2) ZPP
**Inspection Powers**
- Visual inspection permitted in limited circumstances
- Distinct from formal search
- Does not authorize home entry

### Article 211.a ZKP
**Open Area Exception**
- Open/natural spaces not protected as "home"
- Courtyards, fields, etc. may not require warrant
- Applies to "natural space" under paragraph 2
