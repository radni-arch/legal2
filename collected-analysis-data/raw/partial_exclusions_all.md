# Partial Evidence Exclusions

Cases where courts excluded some evidence but retained other portions.

---

## 1. I Kž 181/10-3 (Vrhovni sud)

**Date:** 2010-05-12
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Bedroom search records | EXCLUDED |
| Bathroom search records | EXCLUDED |
| Kitchen search records | RETAINED |

### Reason
Witnesses testified they were **not continuously present** during bedroom and bathroom searches. Kitchen search had proper witness presence throughout.

### Principle
Witness presence must be **continuous** - brief absences during search can invalidate that portion.

---

## 2. Kž-210/2017-7 (Županijski sud u Puli)

**Date:** 2017-12-19
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Defendant's phone call logs | EXCLUDED |
| Defendant's SMS messages | EXCLUDED |
| Victim's phone evidence | RETAINED |

### Reason
Defendant's phone data obtained unlawfully. Court noted **fruit of poisonous tree** issue regarding victim's phone but first-instance ruling on victim's evidence stood.

### Open Question
Whether victim's phone evidence derived from unlawful search of defendant's phone - not fully resolved.

---

## 3. Kž-270/2022-10 (Županijski sud u Bjelovaru)

**Date:** 2022-09-21
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Audio recording of conversation | EXCLUDED |
| Photographs from search | RETAINED |
| Witness statements | RETAINED |

### Reason
Audio recording made **without consent or judicial authorization** violated Article 10(2) ZKP. Photographs derived from lawful warrant-based search.

### Key Distinction
Private recordings require either:
- Consent of recorded party, OR
- Judicial authorization under Art. 332 ZKP

---

## 4. 5 Kž-720/2017-3 (Županijski sud u Zagrebu)

**Date:** 2017-09-05
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Call duration/frequency data | EXCLUDED |
| IMEI numbers | EXCLUDED |
| Phone owner identification | RETAINED |

### Reason
**Article 339 ZKP** requires judge's order for telecommunication metadata. Paragraphs 3-4 of police report (call logs, IMEI) obtained without such order.

### Principle
Phone subscriber identity ≠ call metadata. Subscriber info may be obtainable without special order; call details require judicial authorization.

---

## 5. I Kž-Us-57/2020-4 (Vrhovni sud)

**Date:** 2020-09-10
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION / REMAND

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Home search of M.M. | REMANDED |
| Warehouse search records | RETAINED |
| Witness statements | RETAINED |

### Reason
First-instance court failed to conduct contradictory proceedings regarding M.M.'s home search and ignored relevant prior judgment. Other evidence properly obtained.

---

## 6. III Kr 165/11-5 (Vrhovni sud)

**Date:** 2012-09-19
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Sharp 8 MB memory card | EXCLUDED |
| Apartment search records | RETAINED |
| Laptop search records | RETAINED |
| Vehicle search records | RETAINED |
| TDK CDs | RETAINED |

### Reason
Specific memory card search lacked proper judicial order. Other searches conducted under valid orders with appropriate procedures.

---

## 7. I Kž-Us 61/12-4 (Vrhovni sud)

**Date:** 2012-07-03
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| USKOK questioning record | EXCLUDED |
| Search records | RETAINED |
| Forensic reports | RETAINED |
| Mobile/computer searches | RETAINED |

### Reason
Attorney D.C. lacked **required minimum 10 years of practice** under applicable law for USKOK proceedings. Other evidence derived from lawful searches.

### Principle
Defense counsel qualifications are substantive requirements - unqualified representation voids the proceeding.

---

## Pattern Analysis

### What Gets Excluded
- Audio recordings without consent/authorization
- Telecom metadata without judge's order
- Search portions without continuous witness presence
- Statements taken with unqualified counsel
- Specific items seized without proper individual authorization

### What Gets Retained
- Evidence from separate lawful searches
- Witness statements obtained independently
- Photographs from properly authorized procedures
- Items covered by valid search orders

---

## 8. I Kž-1019/2010-4 (Vrhovni sud)

**Date:** 2010
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Bedroom search records | EXCLUDED |
| Bathroom search records | EXCLUDED |
| Living room search records | RETAINED |

### Reason
Witnesses not continuously present during bedroom and bathroom searches. Living room search properly witnessed.

---

## 9. Kžm-31/2020-4 (Vrhovni sud)

**Date:** 2020
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Mobile phone photographs | EXCLUDED |
| Informal interview notes | EXCLUDED |
| Formal witness statements | RETAINED |

### Reason
Mobile photos obtained through informal interview procedure violation. Formal witness statements obtained with proper procedures.

---

## 10. I Kž-110/2011-4 (Vrhovni sud)

**Date:** 2011
**Outcome:** PARTIAL EXCLUSION

| Evidence | Status |
|----------|--------|
| Witness testimony (unauthorized police conversation portions) | EXCLUDED |
| Witness testimony (proper examination portions) | RETAINED |

### Reason
Court redacted portions of testimony that referenced unauthorized police conversations while retaining properly obtained testimony.

---

## Pattern Analysis

### What Gets Excluded
- Audio recordings without consent/authorization
- Telecom metadata without judge's order
- Search portions without continuous witness presence
- Statements taken with unqualified counsel
- Specific items seized without proper individual authorization

### What Gets Retained
- Evidence from separate lawful searches
- Witness statements obtained independently
- Photographs from properly authorized procedures
- Items covered by valid search orders
