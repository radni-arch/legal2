Based on everything we've analyzed in your case (Pp Prz-74/2025), here are the specific violations:
ECHR Convention — Violated Articles
Članak 6 § 1 — pravo na pošteno suđenje
The core violation. Denial of access to the search warrant file destroys equality of arms — you can't verify the lawfulness of the search or prepare an effective defense. The informal email denials without reasoned decisions compound this. Key precedent: Garcia Alva v. Germany.
Članak 8 — pravo na poštovanje privatnog i obiteljskog života, doma i dopisivanja
The search of your home is a direct interference with Article 8 rights. Without access to the warrant file, you cannot verify whether the interference met the Article 8 § 2 test — "in accordance with the law" and "necessary in a democratic society." The vague "operativni izvidi" justification and the disproportionate involvement of Služba organiziranog kriminaliteta for a čl.54 st.3 ZSOZD matter make this particularly strong.
Članak 13 — pravo na djelotvorno pravno sredstvo
No formal rješenje was ever issued → nothing to appeal → no effective remedy exists. Email denials from Judge Bertok, the court president, and the County Court all lack reasoning and legal remedy instructions. This is textbook Article 13.
You could also argue Članak 18 (granice primjene ograničenja prava) — that the restrictions on your rights are being applied for purposes other than those prescribed, especially if the pretextual nature of the warrant holds up.
Ustav RH — Violated Articles
Članak 18 — pravo na žalbu
"Jamči se pravo na žalbu" — yet there's nothing to appeal because no formal decision was ever issued. This is the procedural trap they've put you in.
Članak 29 — pravično suđenje
Fair trial requires reasoned decisions and access to case materials. You got informal emails. The Constitutional Court in U-III-3071/2006 already established that parties must not suffer consequences from courts' failure to follow proper procedures.
Članak 34 — nepovredivost doma
Requires an "obrazloženi pisani nalog" — a reasoned written warrant. A warrant that says only "operativni izvidi" and references a secret "nulti dokument" arguably fails the constitutional standard of being properly reasoned.
Članak 14 — jednakost pred zakonom (if applicable)
If you can show similarly situated persons received different treatment in file access.
Članak 22 — jednakost pred zakonom / Članak 19 — zakonitost uprave
The courts' refusal to issue formal decisions violates basic rule-of-law principles embedded in these articles.
The strongest trifecta for both the ustavna tužba and ECHR application remains Articles 6+8+13 ECHR mirrored by Articles 18+29+34 Ustav RH — they create an interlocking argument where each violation reinforces the others.
